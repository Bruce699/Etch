OpenAI: The New Moat
The End of Model-Based Moats
LLM quality is converging; foundational models are increasingly commoditized.
Being first-mover gave OpenAI early advantage, but the gap is shrinking.
If the model itself is no longer the moat → where does the defensibility come from?
The New Shift: Experience, Ecosystem, and Defaults
Frontier models no longer act as the differentiator they once were.
Everyone ships:
deep research tools
code assistants
voice agents
image/video generation
agentic task runners
Experiences across providers are converging.
Distribution advantage lies with Google, Apple, Microsoft, not OpenAI.
Key insight: If quality converges, the moat must move up the stack.
Why OpenAI Needs a New Moat
First-mover advantage and “best model” advantage were temporary.
The space looks more like industrial economics than software:
High variable compute costs.
Scaling revenue ≠ scaling margins.
Harder for smaller players to compete against distribution giants.
OpenAI needs a moat that:
Reinforces usage,
Reduces switching costs,
And compounds in value over time.
That moat is identity.
The Case for AI Identities
Users already treat ChatGPT as “the AI that knows me”
This is the closest thing to a persistent digital self that people have ever used.
AI that knows you becomes irreplaceable if it understands your: context, projects, relationships, taste, habits, working patterns, then switching providers becomes as painful as switching brains.
